My Story

I'm Ingkar Shokan, from Almaty, Kazakhstan. I got full financial aid at a US college as an international student, and this is what that process actually looked like.

International students aren't eligible for US federal aid, so any "full aid" has to come from the colleges themselves.

  • Knox College offered me a Presidential Scholarship worth $43,000 a year for four years.
  • Kenyon College admitted me with full financial aid. Kenyon meets full demonstrated need for every student it admits, international or not.

I chose Kenyon: it covered my full need, and its science and statistics programs fit what I wanted to study.

Full aid for international students is real and more available than most people assume. Almost nobody explains, step by step, how to find and win it. That's why I started inkadmissions.
