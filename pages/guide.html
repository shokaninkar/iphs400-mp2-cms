The Guide

"Full aid" is a bigger category than the handful of famous need-blind schools everyone applies to.

1. Know the three categories of aid

  • Need-blind: admission without considering ability to pay. A short, very selective list.
  • Need-aware, full need met: ability to pay may count in admissions, but admitted students get their full demonstrated need covered. Kenyon is one.
  • Merit scholarships: awarded for achievement, independent of need. Knox's Presidential Scholarship is one.

2. Build your list across all three

Applying only to the famous need-blind schools is the most common mistake.

3. Track two sets of deadlines

Financial aid deadlines are often separate from admissions deadlines.

Aid policies change every year. Always confirm a school's current policy with its financial aid office.
