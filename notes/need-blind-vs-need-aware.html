Need-blind vs. need-aware: what's the actual difference

2026-10-16

Need-blind schools decide admission without looking at whether you can pay. For international students that list is very short and very selective.

Need-aware schools may consider ability to pay. Some of them still meet full demonstrated need for every student they admit. Kenyon is one.

The second list is much longer and much less competitive, and most students never hear about it.
